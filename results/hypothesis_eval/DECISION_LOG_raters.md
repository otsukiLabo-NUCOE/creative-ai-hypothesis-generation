# Decision log: raters for Experiment 5 (blinded hypothesis evaluation)

**Date of decision:** 2026-10-04

**Decision:** Experiment 5 is analyzed and reported with raters R1 and R2 only. R3 is withdrawn.
The same 30 sets ranked independently by the LLM judge (Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct, run on the desktop PC)
are reported as a supplementary rater.

**Reason:** The inclusion criterion stated in the manuscript is "researchers in AI-related fields".
R1 (information science and computational science) and R2 (information and communication technology)
meet this criterion. R3, who is in econometrics, does not.

**Timing (integrity statement):** This decision was made before R3 had started rating, so no R3 data
existed. It was also made before the LLM-judge rankings of the desktop PC were available. The decision
therefore does not depend on any rating outcome. R1's and R2's ratings had already been returned and
inspected. The decision is based only on the field criterion and not on how the R1/R2 results look.

**Actions:**
- R3 is informed that the rating is no longer needed, and is thanked for agreeing to help. R3's rater
  package (rater_package_hypotheses/R3, order_R3.csv) is kept unchanged for transparency but is not used.
- The manuscript (Section 4.6) and the response letters are revised to describe two raters plus the
  supplementary LLM judge.
