# Business Intelligence for Competitive Advantage to Solve Demand Uncertainty
===========================================================

## Abstract

In today's fast-paced business environment, managing demand uncertainty has become a critical challenge for companies seeking to establish a competitive advantage. This paper proposes a Business Intelligence (BI) approach to address demand uncertainty, leveraging the power of data-driven decision-making to inform strategic business planning. By harnessing the potential of BI, organizations can develop a more nuanced understanding of market trends, customer behavior, and internal operations, ultimately driving competitiveness and success. This study aims to contribute to the existing literature by exploring the application of BI in tackling demand uncertainty, with a focus on achieving competitive advantage.

## Introduction

As highlighted in [1], the quality of managerial decisions has a direct impact on business performance, underscoring the importance of reliable, inclusive, and trustworthy data. Business Intelligence (BI) has emerged as a key enabler of better decision-making, transforming data into actionable insights that inform business strategy. However, the literature on BI has largely focused on its application in large enterprises, with smaller businesses often overlooked [3]. This paper seeks to bridge this knowledge gap by examining the potential of BI in addressing demand uncertainty, a pressing concern for businesses of all sizes.
